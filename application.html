    05 — Food safety

    Only if you are selling anything edible or drinkable. You will need current FoodSafe BC certification and Fraser Health approval before you trade, and Serving It Right for every staff member if you are pouring alcohol.

    06 — Show us your work

    Your own photographs, please — not supplier or stock images. JPG or PNG, up to 10MB each.

    07 — Your market experience

    08 — The chalet you would like

    Chalets are assigned first come, first served once your deposit is in. Naming a second choice makes it much more likely we can place you.

    09 — Anything else


    We keep what you send to run the village and nothing else. Because of the number of applications we receive, only successful applicants are contacted — a waitlist is kept and we will tell you if you are on it.
